# Music Assets

Place your background music files in this directory.

## Format

- Format: MP3 or OGG
- Recommended: Loopable instrumental tracks
- Keep file size reasonable for mobile (< 5MB each)

## Music Sources

- **Free Music Archive** (freemusicarchive.org)
- **Incompetech** (incompetech.com) - Royalty-free music
- **YouTube Audio Library** - Free music for creators
- **Bensound** (bensound.com) - Free music with attribution

## Rock Theme Ideas

For a rock star theme, look for:
- Rock instrumentals
- Electric guitar tracks
- Upbeat energetic music
- Retro 80s rock vibes

## License

Ensure any music you use has appropriate licensing for your use case.
For this personal/educational project, look for Creative Commons licensed music.
